## Volume overview

Fourteen-year-old **Kaito** doodles constantly but rarely finishes anything—unfinished work cannot be judged. When he and his friend **Ren** find a narrow stationery shop, keeper **Mira** lends him a mysterious **Inkwell** under three rules: finish what you start, own what appears, and never pretend the ink is endless.

Kaito’s carefully finished fox, **Sumi**, becomes an Echo—a drawing that can step into reality. Wonder follows, then consequence: a fear-driven unfinished “guardian” sketch leaks into the neighbourhood as **The Unfinished**. With Ren’s help and Mira’s refusal to finish the line for him, Kaito enters the drawing-space, completes the creature with honest intent, and learns that creativity without responsibility is only half a story.

## Chapter summaries

### Chapter 1 — Ordinary Lines (pages 1–8)

Kaito’s bedroom sketch habit, walk through the neighbourhood, school life with Ren, and the pull toward a shop he somehow never noticed. Establishes unfinished-drawing avoidance, friendship banter, and the art-festival pressure seed.

### Chapter 2 — The Shop Between Pages (pages 9–16)

Mira introduces the Inkwell and its rules. Kaito borrows it. At night he opens the lid and begins redrawing his unfinished fox with deliberate care—the page begins to listen.

### Chapter 3 — First Echo (pages 17–24)

Sumi crosses into reality. Bond and limitations appear (focus, rest-on-page, ink thinning, headache cost). Kaito closes the lid respectfully. Wonder is real; power is not unlimited.

### Chapter 4 — Consequences Ink (pages 25–34)

Festival signup panic drives a jagged unfinished “protector” sketch. The Unfinished leaks out, escapes erasure, and marks the streets. Ren discovers Sumi and the secret. Midpoint: the large silhouette is sighted. Mira affirms only the creator can finish it. Ink is nearly gone—one chance remains.

### Chapter 5 — The Line That Holds (pages 35–42)

Crisis at the school fence: Ren endangered, Sumi shields. Kaito opens the drawing-space storm, faces The Unfinished’s hunger to be completed, fails at blacking-out, then redraws protection without panic. Friendship steadies the last drop of ink. The Unfinished becomes a calm guardian; the storm clears.

### Chapter 6 — Echoes Remain (pages 43–50)

Neighbourhood marks fade. Creations rest finished on the page. Mira allows stewardship with slow refill—never endless ink. Kaito finishes a festival piece the ordinary way, accepts praise, walks home with Ren, and Mira hints other tools wait. Final peaceful bedroom image.
